# POLARNEXA

## Integrated Polar Expedition Logistics and Asset Management System

POLARNEXA is an integrated, offline-first platform designed to support
polar expedition logistics, personnel, cargo, assets, inventory,
transportation and emergency response.

## 🚀 Problem

Polar expeditions operate in remote environments with limited
connectivity and complex logistics.

Information about:

- Cargo
- Personnel
- Assets
- Inventory
- Transportation
- Weather
- Emergency resources

can become fragmented across different systems.

POLARNEXA brings these operations together into a unified platform.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

POLARNEXA provides:

- **Smart Expedition Planner** – Plans resources based on mission duration and personnel.
- **Cargo & Asset Tracking** – Tracks location, status and maintenance.
- **Predictive Inventory** – Forecasts shortages and resupply needs.
- **Personnel & Emergency Dashboard** – Monitors readiness and emergency resources.
- **GIS + Offline Sync** – Supports mapping and operation during poor connectivity.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- Frontend: React.js

- Database: MySQL
- Maps: GIS / Map-based visualization
- Optimization: OR-Tools
- Offline Storage: IndexedDB
- APIs: REST APIs
